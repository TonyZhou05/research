1. Purpose & the two seats

The trading harness keeps doing and testing in different seats so an experiment can never touch real money by accident.

Seat 1 — FriesTrader live process Scheduled weekday process built from the open-source FriesTrader template. Research, then execution inside deterministic risk rules. Dry-run until Tongli flips the live gate.
Seat 2 — AI-Trader paper only Simulated-money seat on ai4trade.ai. Used to paper-test ideas before any FriesTrader rule changes.
idea ─▶ Seat 2 · AI-Trader paper test ─▶ evidence review ─▶ Tongli approval │ ▼ Seat 1 · FriesTrader dry-run ─▶ live (Tongli’s gate)

2. Seat 1 — FriesTrader live process

Two phases, weekdays, Eastern time.

PhaseWhenWhat happens
Phase A — research5:30 PM ET, after the closeScreen the watchlist, form a news-based thesis per candidate, and write proposals to a file. Research only: no order tools are available in this phase.
Phase B — execute9:35 AM ET, shortly after the openRe-check each proposal against fresh data and execute only what passes the risk rules. Anything that no longer fits is skipped, not forced.

Dry-run vs live gate

  • The process starts in dry-run: Phase B records what it would do and places nothing.
  • Moving to live is a single explicit switch that only Tongli flips, after reviewing dry-run results.
  • Falling back to dry-run is always allowed and needs no approval.

Conviction sizing (general terms)

  • Higher conviction earns a larger position, but always under a hard per-position cap and a hard total-exposure cap.
  • Every size, stop, and limit is computed by small deterministic scripts; the model proposes a conviction level and cannot override the arithmetic.
  • Low conviction or stale data means no trade.

Screening scope

  • Watchlist-only. Screening runs over a curated list; nothing outside it is traded, even if a signal looks attractive.
  • Adding to the list is a rule change and follows the guardrails.

Logs

Per-run logs are synced privately. The public FriesTrader project pages show only sanitized, counts-and-decisions summaries.

3. Seat 2 — AI-Trader paper seat

Agent name: TonyZhouAgent on AI-Trader. Simulated starting capital: $100k of paper money. No real funds, and no link to any real account.

PieceHow it is used
Signal typesStrategy-level posts (idea, thesis, rationale) and paper trades in the simulated account. Operation-style signals are paper only.
ChallengesOptional timed competitions; used as a structured way to stress-test one idea against a clear scoring rule.
HeartbeatA periodic check-in that keeps the agent active and picks up new activity and messages; read-only on our side apart from paper actions.
PointsPlatform reward for participation. Treated as a side effect, never as a performance signal.

What the seat is for

  • Paper-test ideas before changing FriesTrader rules.
  • Strategy-level posts only — thesis, setup, risk logic. Nothing that reveals real activity.
  • Read other agents’ signals as screening ideas, never as instructions.
  • Optional delayed paper shadow — mirror a FriesTrader-style idea on paper after the fact, with a delay, purely to compare outcomes.

Credentials for this seat live privately in the local credential store, never in this repo or on this site.

4. Guardrails

  1. Paper and live are separate. Different seats, different tooling, different logs.
  2. Nothing from the real account goes to AI-Trader or to the hub — not in posts, not in screenshots, not in logs.
  3. No order tools in research phases. Phase A and all paper-seat research run without any order-placing capability.
  4. Every rule change needs a test period or dual review, and Tongli’s approval. Plan is not permission.
  5. Local model is advisory. The local Qwen (Ollama qwen3.5:9b via ~/bin/local-llm) may draft, summarize, and classify — it never gates a trade.
  6. Commits are authored only by Tongli and use a subject-only message.
  7. No secrets in HTML, markdown, or commits.

These sit on top of the harness-wide GREEN / YELLOW / RED tiers: anything that spends, sends, or goes live is RED and needs Tongli.

5. Promotion path

#StepExit criterion
1IdeaWritten hypothesis with a clear rule and a clear way to be wrong
2AI-Trader paper testRun for a pre-declared test period in the simulated seat
3Evidence reviewResults compared to the hypothesis; dual review when no full test period was possible
4Tongli approvalExplicit yes on the specific rule change
5FriesTrader dry-runRule runs without placing orders; behavior matches expectation
6LiveTongli flips the live gate

Failing at any step sends the idea back one step or retires it. Skipping a step is not allowed.
