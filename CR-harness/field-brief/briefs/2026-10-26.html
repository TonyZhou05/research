1. DECE watch

What it is. arXiv:2610.35773 (submitted 26 Oct 2026). DECE (Diverse Eurogame Competitive Environments) is a unified arena of ten executable Eurogames. Every game exposes setup, seat-specific observations, legal actions, state transitions and terminal rankings through one interface; the engine enforces rules and scoring, and MCP or native adapters plug in four execution harnesses. Persisted states and execution records support audit, replay and resume.

Evidence. 12 model–harness configurations (nine models, four harnesses) over 480 three-player matches (1,032 matches across all studies). Cross-game rankings broadly agree, but matched studies show the same model performing differently across harnesses, and performance advantages do not consistently translate into token or cost efficiency.

Why it matters for CR. Closest public design to the CR-Harness contract: engine-owned legal_actions, frozen state for simultaneous choices, replayable records. It also argues we should report model+harness pairs (and cost) rather than model-only scores.

Suggested next step

  • Mirror DECE’s matched-harness study on CR: fix seeds and seats, swap only the harness layer, and log cost per match alongside win rate.
