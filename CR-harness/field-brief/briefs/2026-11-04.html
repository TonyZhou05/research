1 · RTSGameBench / RTSGameAgent: command DAGs that run between model calls

watch arXiv:2606.18950 (v4). A VLM benchmark on the open-source RTS Beyond All Reason with full games, five diagnostic mini-games and a self-evolving game generator. The harness groups units and has the model emit, per group, a condition-dependent command trajectory (a DAG) whose transitions fire on the group’s own execution state without another model call; predefined triggers force an early replan on critical events. In the Lv.2 duel ablation, win rate falls from 0.43 to 0.13 without trajectory planning and to 0.07 without group-level control; removing replanning costs less (0.33). The game pauses during inference, so this measures decision quality, not reaction time.

For CR: Clash Royale does not pause. A small plan with explicit fire-conditions (“if elixir ≥ X and lane pressure, place Y”) executed by the harness between decision_point calls is the same idea, and the ablation says the conditional follow-ups and triggers, not memory, carry most of the gain. Worth a harness card next to the real-time cards already on the board.

2 · StepGate: escalate one step, not the whole query

watch arXiv:2610.07816. A calibrated gate scores each local small-model action from token uncertainty, tool margin, schema validity, sample consistency, trajectory state and a risk tier, then executes locally, retries once, or hands that single step to a stronger model with a minimized context. On 100 scripted multi-turn tasks (Qwen2.5-1.5B local, 7B strong) it reaches 69.0% trajectory success at 30% cloud actions vs 48.0% local-only, 57.0% query-level routing and 82.0% strong-only. The authors flag the small, single-family evaluation and that the gain over random escalation (+9.0) is not significant.

For CR: this is the Jev router question at decision_point granularity: keep the fast model on routine placements and escalate only uncertain or high-stakes steps. The cheap signals (legal-action validity, agreement across a few samples, menu margin) are ones the harness can already log, so the gate could be fit offline from ETL replays before anything runs live.
