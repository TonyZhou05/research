Dattri-llm

Efficient training-data attribution (TDA) infrastructure for LLM-scale models. Dattri-llm attributes model behavior back to individual training examples with factorized per-sample gradients, hook-based capture, and flexible TracIn / K-FAC / EK-FAC / DVEmb workflows. It wraps existing training contexts, including Hugging Face Trainer and OLMo, rather than rewriting the loop.
