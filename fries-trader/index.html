FriesTrader

A scheduled AI trading agent for a small, dedicated Robinhood Agentic account. Claude Code sessions screen US stocks and write a news-based thesis; every size, stop and limit is computed by small deterministic Python scripts the model cannot override. Built from the open-source YizhiSong/FriesTrader template.

How the pipeline works

  • Phase A: screen & thesis (weekdays after close, ~4:30pm CT). Starts from a Robinhood watchlist, a saved scan and current holdings. Filters out illiquid, penny, leveraged/inverse and below-200-day-average names. Only stocks with a notable signal (a big 60-day move, a volume spike, or near a 52-week extreme) get a news search. The model then writes a long / avoid / exit call with a rubric-based conviction (high / medium / low). Places no orders.
  • Phase B: re-verify & enforce (weekdays shortly after the open, ~8:35am CT). Sells run first (stop-loss, take-profit, trims, exits). Buys must then pass an entry gate (not too far above the thesis price or the 20-day average, and no wash-sale or re-entry lock). Candidates are ranked by conviction and sized by fixed percentages. Every order is previewed first; in dry run it is only logged.

Risk rules, in plain words

  • Position size is a fixed share of the account per conviction tier, capped per position, with a maximum number of open positions and a minimum cash buffer.
  • Stop-loss scales with each stock’s recent volatility, clamped to a band. After any profit-taking it trails the high instead of the entry price. Note: it is checked once per trading day, not as a resting order.
  • Take-profit sells a quarter of the position at each of three gain tiers.
  • Loss limits halt new buys after a daily or weekly drawdown threshold.
  • Live-order gate: a real order needs mode = "live" (set only by the human), at least 10 logged dry-run days, and a clean broker preview.
